About Jenn

Jenn Kanellis

Advisor

Jenn Kanellis holds a BA in Applied Psychology and an MS in School Counseling, and is a Certified Career Development Facilitator. She brings more than 20 years as a college enrollment expert and strategist, more than 13 years as a private college coach, experience as a former Chief Enrollment Officer, over 1,000 counseling hours logged, and partnerships with more than 300 colleges across the U.S.

While college is not for everyone, the research stands the test of time: higher education leads to a happier, healthier, and more successful individual and society. Individuals with a two- or four-year degree, on average, earn more and live longer. Jenn has made it her mission to help more students see college as a viable option and help them choose their best college fit.

With nearly 20 years of enrollment strategy and leadership experience, Jenn has partnered with over 300 institutions in the U.S. to drive enrollment, student retention, and graduation rates. Her expertise is rooted in counseling: understanding the unique strengths and interests of each student to help them map out their education plan to achieve their career aspirations.

Jenn is a proud wife and mom of three awesome kids. When she is not focused on college enrollment, she is with her family outdoors: on the lake, on the golf course, or on the slopes.
