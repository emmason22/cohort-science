The Shift

Higher education is undergoing a fundamental structural transformation.

Demographic shifts, credential disruption, workforce realignment, and evolving capital flows are redefining how institutions grow, how investors evaluate the sector, and how consulting firms serve their clients.

Cohort Science™ is purpose-built to address this challenge, providing the analytical depth and speed of delivery that consequential decisions demand.

The Idea

A new foundation for education intelligence.

Cohort Science™ maintains a continuously updated intelligence base spanning labor markets, student demand, program economics, graduate outcomes, and geographic market dynamics.

Clients We Serve

Sector Intelligence Built for Every Decision-Maker

Institutional Leaders

Private and public colleges, faith-based institutions, and community colleges rely on Cohort Science™ to provide institutional leaders with a clear, continuous view of the student lifecycle, from recruitment through graduation and beyond. Designed for presidents, provosts, deans, and enrollment leaders, our platform transforms complex data into actionable intelligence that drives enrollment growth, strengthens student success, closes equity gaps, and demonstrates measurable outcomes to boards, accreditors, and strategic partners.

Foundations and Impact Investors

Today's most effective philanthropy is driven by evidence. Cohort Science™ equips foundations, impact investors, and mission-driven grantmakers with independent, outcomes-based intelligence that measures program effectiveness, graduate success, workforce mobility, and long-term institutional impact. By moving beyond self-reported metrics, our platform helps funders allocate capital with greater confidence, transparency, and measurable social return.

Non-Profit and Mission-Driven Organizations

Organizations dedicated to expanding educational access and creating lasting pathways to opportunity require more than reporting. They need a trusted framework for measuring and amplifying impact. Cohort Science™ provides the analytical infrastructure to quantify student success, workforce outcomes, and community transformation, enabling nonprofits and mission-driven organizations to build enduring partnerships with funders, institutions, and employers. As expectations for accountability and measurable impact continue to grow, our platform helps organizations not only demonstrate value today, but shape the future of educational and economic mobility for generations to come.

How Our Products Help Clients

Market Signal™

Forecast Demand. Optimize Growth.

  • Predict enrollment demand and revenue potential for new and existing academic programs using market and demographic intelligence
  • Identify high-value student markets by geography, population, and program affinity to improve recruitment effectiveness

Education Outcomes Platform™

Measure Success Beyond Graduation.

  • Track graduate employment, earnings, and career progression across institutions, programs, and graduating cohorts
